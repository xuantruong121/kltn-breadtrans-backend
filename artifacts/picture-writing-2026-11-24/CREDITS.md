# Pictures of the second picture-writing batch (2026-11-24)

Twenty new "Viết câu theo tranh" tasks (owner 24/11: "sinh thêm số lượng nhiều bài nữa", +20, real
CC0/PD photos, mostly free). All are CC0 or public domain: no credit is required, it is recorded
anyway.

How they were chosen:
- Searched with the Openverse and Wikimedia Commons APIs, licence filter `cc0,pd`, landscape, at
  least 1200 px wide.
- Each image was opened and checked by eye before use.
- rawpixel results were all dropped, because their downloadable copies carry a visible watermark.
- Pictures used by the TOEIC exams were avoided, so practice and exam content stay apart: the D2
  set in `artifacts/toeic-fix-d2` (restaurant kitchen, airport check-in, market pumpkins, truck
  unloading, bus stop) and the Part 1 images.

Cropped to 4:3 and never enlarged, at most 1448×1086 like the first ten pictures. JPEG quality 86.
StockSnap serves 960 px copies, so those pictures are 853×640 (clothes-shop 720×540).
